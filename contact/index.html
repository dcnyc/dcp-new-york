I’m a portrait photographer based in New York and New Jersey, specializing in headshots, lifestyle, corporate, and event photography.

Before photography, I spent years as a professional musician, performing in clubs, Broadway pits, and alongside leading classical artists. That experience taught me a lot about working with people, reading a room, and recognizing the right moment—skills that have translated naturally to photography.

My approach is straightforward: natural expression, clean composition, and images that feel authentic. Whether I’m photographing a corporate executive, an artist, an event, or someone who simply needs a great headshot, I want the experience to be comfortable and the results to feel like you.

My work has been featured on blogs and social media, and I’ve had the opportunity to photograph for an international menswear brand.
